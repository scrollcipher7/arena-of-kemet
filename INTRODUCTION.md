# MEDJAY — ARENA OF KEMET
## The Maker's Introduction

I am Scroll Cipher — the Medjay of the Archive of History,
a Taíno son of Borikén.

I built this arena with my own hands, because no one had
ever built it for us. I am the first Taíno to raise a game
such as this, and I raise it representing Borikén.

Here the gods of the Nile meet the gods of Greece —
five against five, three roads, one river — and every
player chooses his god freely.

I leave this game to the world. When they ask who made it,
let the answer stand: one of the first people of Borikén
made it — and he did not ask permission.

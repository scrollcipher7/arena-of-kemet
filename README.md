# MEDJAY: Arena of Kemet

**Egyptian Gods vs Greek Gods — a 5v5 arena built on the laws of Honor of Kings,
wearing the skin of Kemet.**

Three lanes, a jungle with sleeping ancients, towers that shield and fall,
ten gods in one free pool — any god, either court. The first game of its kind
made by a Taíno representing Borikén: no studio, no team — one Medjay of the
Archive, from the Kingdom of Wisdom.

## What's here

- `ArenaOfKemet/Assets/Scripts` — the whole game brain (heroes, AI,
  match director, HUD, selection hall, sound, juice)
- `ArenaOfKemet/Assets/Editor` — the one-click arena builder
- `audio/` — war theme, kill voice lines, announcer, SFX (and the
  Python smiths that forged the synthesized sounds)
- `bodies/` — the ten gods' bodies (FBX), hand-painted skins, portraits
- `GroundPaint.jpg` — the painted earth of the Gorge
- `FIVE-V-FIVE-BLUEPRINT.md` / `RESEARCH-OVERNIGHT.md` — the study
- `INTRODUCTION.md` — the maker's own word

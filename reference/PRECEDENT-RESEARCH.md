# Deep Research: Precedent for an Egyptian-Gods-vs-Greek-Gods MOBA in an Ancient-Kemet World

## Summary

**Bottom line:** The concept "Egyptian gods battling Greek gods" is **not** new — SMITE (Hi-Rez, 2014) has had Egyptian gods fighting Greek gods in a multi-pantheon MOBA since launch, and its pre-release pitch (2011) literally opened with "Who would win in a fight, Zeus or Anubis?" What **is** genuinely unprecedented, as far as the public record shows, is a **5v5 MOBA whose entire world — map, towers, river, bases — is ancient Kemet** (Nile, obelisks, pyramids, temples), with teams framed as **Egyptian gods versus Greek gods** across one arena:

1. **SMITE/SMITE 2** — multi-pantheon roster games (Egypt is one of 15+ pantheons), not Egypt-themed worlds. Egyptian gods exist and fight Greek gods there, but the game is not *about* Egypt.
2. **SMITE's only Egypt-themed arenas** were side-mode variants, not the whole game: the 5v5 **Clash map** (Egyptian-themed, Feb 2017–Jan 2022, since retired from standard rotation) and **Slash** (Maya+Egyptian inspired, Jan 2022–present) — and even there, any pantheon's gods could be picked; teams were never "Egypt vs Greece."
3. **No other MOBA** (LoL, Dota 2, HotS, Honor of Kings, Arena of Valor, Mobile Legends) has an Egypt-themed map or an Egyptian god roster. LoL only has Egyptian-inspired champions (Shurima) and a temporary 2014 desert event map; Mobile Legends has one Egyptian-themed hero (Khufra).
4. **No indie or announced video game** found that is a 5v5 MOBA/arena battler with an Egypt-themed world and Egyptian-vs-Greek framing. The closest is *Fight of Gods* (2017), a 1v1 2D fighter with a multi-pantheon roster (incl. Anubis and Zeus) on non-Egyptian stages — not a MOBA.
5. **Board games:** *Kemet* (2012) and *Ankh: Gods of Egypt* (2021) are Egyptian-gods-at-war, Egyptian-only. *Mythic Battles: Pantheon* (2017), *Cyclades* (2009), *Santorini* (2016) are Greek-only. The brand-new *Mythic Battles: Isfet* (Kickstarter 2024, Egyptian) pairs Egypt with Norse options, not Greek. **No board game found pits Egyptian gods against Greek gods.**
6. **Other video games:** *Age of Mythology* (2002; *Retold* 2024) has an Egyptian civilization (worship Ra/Isis/Set) but is an RTS with no playable god champions; *AC Origins* (2017) uses ancient Egypt purely as setting; *Immortals Fenyx Rising* (2020) is Greek-only.

**Accurate claim that can be stated:** No 5v5 MOBA has ever been built *as* ancient Kemet — with an all-Egyptian world and Egyptian gods facing Greek gods across one arena. Egyptian gods *appearing* in god-battlers (SMITE, 2014) is old news; Egypt *being the arena itself*, with the two pantheons as opposing sides, has not been done.

**Confidence:** High for video games (multiple corroborating sources per claim); high for board games (publisher press + BGG-adjacent sources). All findings are index-level (no live browser verification performed); the key structural claims are corroborated by 2+ independent sources each.

## Findings

### 1. SMITE and SMITE 2 (Hi-Rez / Titan Forge)

**Multi-pantheon roster, not Egypt-themed.**
- SMITE is a third-person MOBA (first released March 25, 2014) with 100+ mythological gods across 15+ pantheons: Greek, Roman, Egyptian, Norse, Mayan, Hindu, Chinese, Japanese, Celtic, Slavic, Arthurian, Babylonian, Yoruba, Polynesian, Voodoo, Great Old Ones, Tales of Arabia. (index — https://store.steampowered.com/app/386360/SMITE/ ; https://www.pockettactics.com/smite/characters)
- Pre-release coverage (Kotaku, 2011) framed the pitch as "Zeus or Anubis?" — Egyptian-vs-Greek combat was the marketing hook from day one. (index — https://kotaku.com/the-gods-must-be-defending-the-ancients-in-hi-rezs-smit-5794406)
- **Egyptian pantheon in SMITE 1 (15 gods):** Anhur, Anubis, Bastet, Eset, Geb, Horus, Khepri, Neith, Nut, Osiris, Ra, Serqet, Set, Sobek, Thoth. Horus and Set joined simultaneously in April 2019 (SMITE's first-ever dual god release). (index — https://smite.fandom.com/wiki/Anubis ; https://blog.playstation.com/?p=210015)
- **SMITE 2** (closed alpha Aug 27, 2024; free-to-play open beta Jan 2025): Egyptian gods carried over include Anhur, Anubis (alpha launch), plus Geb, Neith, Ra, Sobek, Khepri by open beta (roster ~45 gods). Still multi-pantheon. (index — https://www.escapistmagazine.com/all-playable-gods-in-smite-2/ ; https://dotesports.com/smite/news/all-smite-2-gods-and-goddesses-listed ; https://gamerant.com/smite-2-open-beta-free-to-play-launch/)
- **SMITE 2 maps** announced: an updated Conquest map and a new Arthurian-themed Joust map — no Egypt-themed map. (index — https://gamerant.com/smite-2-open-beta-free-to-play-launch/)

**Egyptian-themed maps did exist — as side-mode variants.**
- **Clash mode (5v5, two lanes):** Season 4 patch 4.1 (Feb 2017) re-skinned it fully Egyptian — pyramids backdrop, Egyptian towers/phoenixes/minions, new jungle boss Apophis; Order side "Nile" feel vs Chaos side "desert pyramid" feel; Anubis and Thoth statues at the center camp; Ra's-glyph pharaoh-statue easter egg. Clash was removed from standard rotation Jan 25, 2022 (patch 9.1; remains in custom matches/SMITE NIGHT). (index — https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2017/01/05/smite-season-4-changes-outlined-celtic-goddess-revealed/ ; https://smite.fandom.com/wiki/SMITE_Version_4.1.3882 ; https://smite.fandom.com/wiki/Clash ; https://www.gamespot.com/articles/smites-next-year-of-content-outlined-includes-a-hu/1100-6446675/)
- **Slash mode (5v5):** launched with Season 9 (Jan 25, 2022); map "inspired by the Maya and Egyptian pantheons," with Apophis as jungle boss. (index — https://www.gamespress.com/SMITE-Season-9-Kicks-Off-Today-with-Slash-Mode-new-Conquest-Map-and-Gl)
- **Critical distinction:** even on Egypt-themed maps, matches were multi-pantheon — any god could be picked; teams were never framed as "Egypt vs Greece," and the main Conquest map (the game's flagship arena) has never been Egyptian (e.g., Season 9's was the "Dharmic Era" map; Arena mode maps are Greco-Roman colosseum themed). (index — same sources as above; https://gameinformer.com/b/news/archive/2014/01/15/smite-rolls-out-new-god-new-map.aspx?amp)

### 5. Video games with playable/represented Egyptian gods generally

- **Age of Mythology** (Ensemble Studios, 2002; *Retold* by World's Edge, Sept 4, 2024): RTS. Egyptian civilization chooses a major god — Ra, Isis, or Set — unlocking minor gods (Bast, Ptah, Anubis, Sobek, Hathor, Sekhmet, Nephthys, Osiris, Horus, Thoth). Gods are worshipped for god powers/myth units, **not played as characters**. Egyptians and Greeks are opposing *civilizations*, not god-arena combatants. (index — https://ageofempires.fandom.com/wiki/Set ; https://steamdb.Info/app/1934680/dlc/)
- **Immortals Fenyx Rising** (Ubisoft Quebec, 2020): open-world action; Greek mythology only — no Egyptian gods.
- **Assassin's Creed Origins** (Ubisoft Montreal, 2017): action RPG set in Ptolemaic Egypt (49 BC); protagonist Bayek the Medjay. Egypt is the *setting* (pyramids, temples, Sobek cult), not a god-battler; Greek (Ptolemaic) rulers are political backdrop. (index — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassin's_Creed_Origins ; https://www.eurogamer.net/assassins-creed-origins-review)
- **SMITE (2014) / SMITE 2 (2024):** playable Egyptian gods in a multi-pantheon MOBA (see §1) — the only true "playable Egyptian gods in a MOBA" precedent.

## The precise novelty distinction (for claim wording)

What has been done:
- ✅ Egyptian gods as playable fighters in a MOBA (SMITE, 2014; SMITE 2, 2024).
- ✅ Egyptian gods vs Greek gods as a matchup inside one game (SMITE, 2014 — literally its pitch).
- ✅ Egypt-themed arena art in a MOBA side mode (SMITE Clash, 2017–2022; Slash, 2022).
- ✅ Egypt-themed desert event map in a MOBA (LoL Ascension, 2014).
- ✅ Egyptian gods at war with each other in board games (*Kemet*, 2012; *Ankh*, 2021; *Mythic Battles: Isfet*, 2024).

What has NOT been done (as far as the public record shows):
- ❌ A MOBA whose **entire world** — map, towers, bases, river, objectives — is ancient Kemet.
- ❌ A MOBA where **team identity itself is "Egypt vs Greece"** across that world.
- ❌ Any board game or video game pitting Egyptian gods against Greek gods as the core premise.

Safe formulation: *"SMITE lets Egyptian gods fight Greek gods, but as one pantheon among many in a pantheon-neutral world — and its Egypt-themed maps were side-mode variants (Clash 2017, Slash 2022) where any god could be picked. No game has ever made ancient Kemet itself the whole arena, with Egyptian gods facing Greek gods across it."*
